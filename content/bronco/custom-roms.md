> [!IMPORTANT]
> [Unlocked Bootloader](/bronco/bootloader) is required to flash custom firmware on the Motorola Thinkphone

Flashing custom firmware replaces the stock one, and usually requires wiping data.
Doing the process first time, be cautious. Make sure that you understand how to recover the phone if something goes wrong.

Officially supported ROMs are maintained by the project itself,
unofficial releases are managed outside the project by third-party developers.

## Android OS
- LineageOS 24.0 (Official, Android 17)
	- Device page: [wiki.lineageos.org/devices/bronco](https://wiki.lineageos.org/devices/bronco/)
	- Flash instructions: [wiki.lineageos.org/devices/bronco/install](https://wiki.lineageos.org/devices/bronco/install/)
	- Bug tracker: [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/LineageOS/issues/issues?q=state%3Aopen%20label%3A%22device%3Abronco%22)
		- Bugs: dt2w, usb-c role change, screen flickering
- Project Infinity X 4.1 (Unofficial, Android 17)
	- Device page: [XDA thread](https://xdaforums.com/t/rom-unofficial-17-ota-project-infinity-x-4-1-for-the-motorola-thinkphone.4805361/)
	- Flash instructions: On XDA
	- Bug tracker:  [GitHub issue tracker](https://github.com/iljasrb/bronco-infinityx/issues) or XDA thread
